1. **Tell us about the kids.** How many and how old, so the books and presents fit.

**Under the steps:** You pay when you send the gift. Free shipping.

# Open questions

1. **Gift deadline.** Today nothing stops someone from buying a gift, or a parent from confirming one, after the Nov 7 lock. When a parent confirms a gift box, it goes to ShipStation right away instead of waiting for the main Hanukkah shipment. So "arrives by Nov 21" only holds if both happen in time. Options:
   - **A.** Set a gift deadline (for example, buy by Nov 7 and confirm by Nov 10), then show it on this page and in the claim email.
   - **B.** Keep selling gifts after Nov 7 and change the copy to "ships within X days".
   - **C.** Leave it open with no stated date.
2. **"Ships with the other Hanukkah boxes."** My Gifts tells parents this once they confirm. Given the point above, that may not be true. Should the warehouse hold gift orders for the main shipment, or should the message change?
3. **Who's the page for?** Headline B says "grandkids". It's the most direct match for the grandparent ads but leaves out aunts, uncles and friends. Pick A, B or C.
4. **Gift credit for bigger families.** $80 credit covers a one-kid box. For two kids the parents pay $10 plus tax. That's stated in the small print above. Alternatively, credit could match the box price: ask how many kids, like the box option does.
