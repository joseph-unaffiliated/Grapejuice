## 3. How it works

**Heading:** How it works

1. **Tell us about the kids.** How many and how old, so the books and presents fit.
2. **Look it over.** We fill the box for you. Swap anything you like, or leave it as is.
3. **Add a note and their email.** We email them that a gift is on the way, with your message.
4. **They confirm where to send it by Nov 7.** They can keep it a surprise (sealed until it arrives) or open it and make small changes. Already know their address? Add it and we'll fill it in for them.

**Under the steps:** You pay when you send the gift. It ships free with all the Hanukkah boxes and arrives by Nov 21.

## 5. Questions

**When will I be charged?**
When you send the gift.

**When will it arrive?**
By Nov 21, well before Hanukkah starts on Dec 5. Gift boxes ship free with all the Hanukkah boxes.

**What's the deadline?**
Nov 7, for both of you: send the gift by Nov 7, and they confirm where to send it by Nov 7.

**Do they need to do anything?**
Yes, one quick step. We email them, they sign in and confirm where to send it by Nov 7. If you add their address, they just confirm it. Either way they can keep it a surprise.

**Can they change what's in it?**
Yes. If they open it, they can swap items. If they add anything beyond what you covered, they pay the difference. They can also turn it into gift credit instead.

**Is a menorah included?**
No. Most families already have one, but you can add one to the box.

**Can I cancel?**
Email [hello@grapejuice.co](mailto:hello@grapejuice.co) and we'll sort it out.

**Do I need an account?**
Yes, a quick sign-up when you send the gift, so you can see when it's been claimed.

# Decisions (Oct 7)

1. **Deadline:** gift boxes can be bought through the Nov 7 lock, and recipients must confirm where to send them by the same lock. The backend enforces both. Gift credit can still be bought after the lock.
2. **Shipping:** confirmed gift boxes are held and go to the warehouse with the main Hanukkah shipment once the lock passes.
3. **Headline:** "Send them Hanukkah in a box".
4. **Gift credit:** matches the box price for the family ($80 plus $10 per additional kid); the form will ask how many kids.
